# Harbor Mutual Insurance - Renters Policy Declarations

Named insured: Dana Reyes. Residence premises: 218 Maple Court, Apt 3B, Springfield. Policy number: HR-2210-5583. Policy period: August 1, 2025 to August 1, 2026, 12:01 a.m. standard time at the residence premises. Policy form: HO-4 Contents Broad Form. Interested party: Maple Court Properties LLC (receives notice of cancellation only).

# Coverages and limits

| Coverage | Limit |
| C - Personal property | $30,000 |
| D - Loss of use (additional living expense) | $9,000 |
| E - Personal liability, each occurrence | $100,000 |
| F - Medical payments to others, each person | $1,000 |

Deductible: $500 per loss, applying to Coverage C only. No deductible applies to Coverages D, E, or F.

Personal property is settled at replacement cost: we pay what it costs to replace an item with a new one of like kind and quality, without subtracting for depreciation. Until the item is actually replaced, we pay actual cash value (its value at the time of loss, after depreciation), and pay the difference once you replace it, if you do so within 180 days of the loss.

Annual premium: $186. Discounts applied: claims-free (10%), monitored smoke alarm (5%), and auto policy with Harbor Mutual (not applied; your auto policy is with another company).

# Perils insured against

We cover sudden and accidental direct physical loss to your personal property caused by: fire or lightning; windstorm or hail; explosion; riot; damage by aircraft or vehicles; smoke; vandalism; theft; falling objects; weight of ice, snow, or sleet; accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam from a plumbing, heating, or air conditioning system or a household appliance; sudden tearing apart or bursting of a water heater or heating system; freezing of plumbing; and sudden damage from an electrical current.

Your property is covered anywhere in the world. Property usually kept at a residence other than the residence premises (such as a vacation home) is covered only up to 10% of Coverage C, or $3,000.

# Special limits of liability

These limits are the most we pay in any one loss for each group of property, and are part of (not in addition to) the Coverage C limit.

| Property | Special limit |
| Cash, bank notes, gift cards, coins | $200 |
| Securities, documents, passports, tickets | $1,500 |
| Jewelry, watches, furs, precious stones - theft only | $1,500 |
| Firearms - theft only | $2,500 |
| Silverware and goldware - theft only | $2,500 |
| Watercraft, including trailers and motors | $1,000 |
| Electronics in or taken from a motor vehicle | $1,500 |
| Business property at the residence premises | $2,500 |
| Bicycles and e-bikes | $1,000 |

Higher limits for jewelry, art, or collections are available by adding a scheduled personal property endorsement.

# Theft away from home

Theft of your property from a locked vehicle is covered when there is visible sign of forced entry. Electronics such as laptops, tablets, and phones stolen from a vehicle are subject to the $1,500 special limit above and the $500 deductible. Property left in an unlocked vehicle, or in plain view in a convertible with the top down, is not covered for theft.

# What is not covered

We do not cover loss caused by: flood, surface water, waves, or tidal water; water that backs up through sewers or drains or overflows a sump pump (unless the Water Backup endorsement is added; it is not on this policy); earthquake or earth movement; mold, fungus, or wet rot, except as a direct result of a covered water loss, up to $5,000; wear and tear, mechanical breakdown, rust, or insects and vermin, including bed bugs; your intentional acts; or power failure that begins away from the residence premises.

We do not cover the building, its fixtures, or the landlord's appliances; your landlord's insurance covers those. We do not cover motor vehicles or their parts, animals, or property of roomers or boarders not related to you.

# Loss of use

If a covered loss makes your apartment unfit to live in, we pay the increase in your living expenses (such as a hotel, meals above your usual cost, and extra mileage) for the shortest reasonable time to repair the apartment or for you to settle elsewhere, up to the Coverage D limit. If a civil authority orders you out because of damage to a neighboring premises from a covered peril, we pay for up to two weeks.

# Personal liability and medical payments

We pay damages for bodily injury or property damage to others for which you are legally liable, up to $100,000 per occurrence, and we defend you against such a claim at our expense. This includes accidental damage you cause to the apartment you rent, such as a kitchen fire you start, but not damage from wear and tear. Medical payments pay reasonable medical expenses of a guest injured at your apartment, up to $1,000 per person, no matter who was at fault. Liability coverage does not apply to injuries to you or your household members, to business activities, or to any motor vehicle.

Dog bites are covered under personal liability, except for the breeds listed in the policy's animal exclusion (the policy lists pit bull terriers, rottweilers, and wolf hybrids).

# What to do after a loss

1. Protect your property from further damage; we pay reasonable costs of temporary repairs.
2. For theft or vandalism, report it to the police within 48 hours and get the report number.
3. Report the claim to us as soon as possible by calling 1-800-555-0187 (claims, 24 hours), or in the Harbor Mutual app.
4. Make a list of damaged or stolen property with what you paid, when you bought it, and receipts or photos if you have them. Keep damaged items until our adjuster has seen them.
5. Send us a signed proof of loss within 60 days of our request.

We will pay a claim within 30 days after we reach agreement with you on the amount.

# Cancellation

You may cancel at any time by calling us; we refund the unearned premium. We may cancel for non-payment with 10 days' notice, or for any reason within the first 60 days with 30 days' notice. Customer service: 1-800-555-0186, Monday to Saturday, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m.
