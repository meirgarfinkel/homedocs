# Northwoods Insurance Company - Homeowners Policy Declarations

Named insured: Dana Reyes. Residence premises: 14 Birch Point Road, Lake Alder (seasonal dwelling). Policy number: NW-HO3-77120. Policy form: HO-3 Special Form with Seasonal Dwelling Endorsement SD-12. Policy period: May 15, 2026 to May 15, 2027. Mortgagee: Lakeside Credit Union, loan 4420-118.

# Coverages and limits

| Coverage | Limit |
| A - Dwelling | $310,000 |
| B - Other structures (detached garage, dock, shed) | $31,000 |
| C - Personal property | $60,000 |
| D - Loss of use | Not covered (seasonal dwelling) |
| E - Personal liability, each occurrence | $300,000 |
| F - Medical payments to others, each person | $5,000 |

# Deductibles

All perils deductible: $1,000 per loss. Windstorm or hail deductible: 2% of the Coverage A limit, which is $6,200 per loss; it applies instead of the all perils deductible to any loss caused by windstorm or hail. Only one deductible applies to a single loss. If a fallen tree damages the dwelling during a windstorm, the windstorm or hail deductible applies.

Annual premium: $1,428, billed in two installments on May 15 and November 15. A $30 discount for the monitored water-leak sensors is applied.

# Seasonal dwelling conditions

This dwelling is not your primary residence and is occupied mainly from May to October. The following conditions are part of this policy.

Heat. From November 1 to April 30, you must maintain heat in the dwelling at 55 degrees or more, or shut off the water supply at the main valve and drain the system and appliances of water. We do not pay for damage from freezing of plumbing, or from water that escapes from frozen pipes, unless you did one of these.

Inspections. While the dwelling is unoccupied, it must be checked in person at least once every 14 days, by you or by someone you choose. Keep a log of the checks; the caretaker's log counts.

Vacancy. If the dwelling has been vacant (empty of the furniture and belongings needed to live there) for more than 60 consecutive days before a loss, we do not cover loss by vandalism, glass breakage, or theft, and we reduce any other covered loss by 15%. A seasonal dwelling that is furnished but unoccupied is not vacant.

Wood stove. The wood stove in the living room must be installed by a professional and its chimney cleaned once a year; keep the receipt. A chimney fire after a missed cleaning is covered, but we may cancel at the next renewal.

# Perils insured against

Coverage A and B: we insure against risk of direct physical loss to the property, except for the losses excluded below. Coverage C: we insure personal property against the named perils listed in the policy, including fire, windstorm, hail, theft, vandalism, and sudden water discharge from a plumbing system.

# Exclusions

We do not cover loss caused by: flood, surface water, or waves, including wave action on the dock (a separate flood policy is needed; the property is in flood zone AE and the mortgagee requires one); ice damage to the dock from lake ice; earth movement; water backup from sewers or drains, unless the Water Backup endorsement is added (it is added: up to $10,000); gradual leaks over 14 days or more; wear and tear, rot, or mold except as part of a covered loss; insects, rodents, or animals owned by you; or damage from any short-term rental activity.

Short-term rentals. Renting the dwelling for fewer than 30 days at a time is not allowed under this policy. If you rent it out through a vacation rental service, you must tell us before the first booking, and a Home-Sharing endorsement is needed.

# Other structures and boats

The dock is covered under Coverage B up to $10,000 of the $31,000 limit, and only for fire, lightning, and windstorm. Boats, motors, and trailers are covered under Coverage C up to $1,500; a separate boat policy is recommended. The 16-foot aluminum fishing boat is insured separately with Lakeside Marine (policy LM-3317).

# Liability

We pay up to $300,000 for each occurrence of bodily injury or property damage to others for which you are legally liable, at the residence premises or elsewhere, plus defense costs. Swimming from the dock is covered. Liability coverage does not apply to watercraft over 25 horsepower, to snowmobiles away from the premises, or to the rental of the premises.

# Reporting a loss

Call 1-877-555-0164 (claims, 24 hours) or use the Northwoods website. Report a burst pipe or roof damage right away and protect the property from further damage: shut off the water, cover the roof with a tarp, or hire a local service; we pay reasonable emergency repair costs, and they count toward the limit. Report theft to the Lake Alder sheriff within 48 hours.

# Caretaker

Caretaker on file: Tom Ellery, 555-0171. He checks the cabin every 10 days from November to April and plows the driveway after any snowfall over 4 inches.
