# RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made between Maple Court Properties LLC, a limited liability company ("Landlord"), and Dana Reyes ("Tenant") for the apartment known as Unit 3B at 218 Maple Court, Springfield (the "Premises"). The Premises include one assigned parking space and one storage locker in the basement, both described below. Landlord and Tenant agree as follows.

# 1. TERM

The Lease begins on August 1, 2025 and ends on July 31, 2026 (the "Term"). After the Term ends, this Lease continues from month to month on the same terms unless either party gives written notice at least 60 days before the end of the Term or, during a month-to-month tenancy, at least 30 days before the end of any month. Landlord will offer a renewal lease no later than 90 days before the end of the Term. Any rent increase on renewal will be stated in that offer.

# 2. RENT

Monthly rent is $1,850, due on the first day of each month. Rent paid after the 5th day of the month is late. A late fee of $75 is charged on the 6th, plus $10 for each additional day the rent remains unpaid, up to a maximum late fee of $150 for any one month. Late fees are additional rent. Rent can be paid through the resident portal (no fee by bank transfer; a 2.9% fee by credit card), by personal check, or by money order. A check returned by the bank for any reason costs $35, and after two returned checks Landlord may require all future rent to be paid by money order or bank transfer.

Partial payments. Landlord may accept a partial payment without giving up the right to collect the rest or to charge late fees on the unpaid part.

# 3. SECURITY DEPOSIT

Tenant has paid a security deposit of $1,850. The deposit is held in a separate account at Springfield Community Bank and does not earn interest for Tenant. The deposit is not a limit on Tenant's responsibility for damage and may not be used by Tenant as the last month's rent.

Landlord will return the deposit, minus any lawful deductions for damage beyond normal wear and tear, unpaid rent, and the move-out charges listed in Schedule A, within 30 days after Tenant moves out and returns all keys, along with an itemized list of any deductions. Tenant must give Landlord a forwarding address in writing. A deposit that cannot be returned because no forwarding address was given will be held for one year.

# 4. UTILITIES AND SERVICES

Landlord pays for water, sewer, and trash collection. Tenant pays for electricity, natural gas, and internet, and must set up these accounts in Tenant's own name before move-in. If a Tenant utility is shut off for non-payment, Landlord may restore it and charge Tenant the cost plus a $50 administrative fee. Recycling is collected every Tuesday; bins are in the rear lot.

# 5. OCCUPANTS AND GUESTS

Only Tenant and the occupants listed on the application may live in the Premises. A guest may stay no more than 14 nights in any six-month period without Landlord's written approval.

# 6. PETS

One cat or one dog under 40 pounds is allowed with Landlord's written approval. A one-time pet fee of $300 (not refundable) and pet rent of $35 per month apply. Tenant is responsible for any damage caused by the pet, and dogs must be leashed in all common areas. Aquariums up to 20 gallons are allowed without approval. Reptiles, rodents other than hamsters, and birds of prey are not allowed.

# 7. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE

Tenant must report needed repairs through the resident portal. Emergencies, such as no heat when the outdoor temperature is below 50 degrees, a gas smell, flooding, a sewage backup, or a broken exterior door lock, should be reported by phone at 555-0199 at any time, day or night. Landlord will respond to emergencies within 24 hours and to ordinary repairs within 7 days.

Tenant is responsible for replacing light bulbs and smoke detector batteries, for keeping drains clear, and for the cost of repairing any damage caused by Tenant, Tenant's guests, or Tenant's pets. Tenant may hang pictures with small nails but may not paint, install shelves, change locks, or make other alterations without Landlord's written permission. The dishwasher, refrigerator, range, and the in-unit washer belong to Landlord; Landlord repairs them unless the damage was caused by misuse.

# 8. RENTERS INSURANCE

Tenant must carry renters insurance with at least $100,000 of personal liability coverage for the whole Term and must name Maple Court Properties LLC as an interested party. Landlord's insurance does not cover Tenant's belongings.

# 9. LANDLORD'S ENTRY

Landlord may enter the Premises to make repairs, to inspect, or to show the Premises to prospective tenants or buyers, with at least 24 hours' notice, between 8 a.m. and 6 p.m. on weekdays. No notice is needed in an emergency. During the last 60 days of the Term, Landlord may show the Premises up to three times a week.

# 10. PARKING AND STORAGE

One assigned parking space (number 14) in the rear lot is included in the rent. Guest parking is limited to 48 hours. A second space, when available, costs $60 per month. Vehicles that do not run or are not registered will be towed at the owner's expense after a 72-hour notice. Storage locker B-7 in the basement is included; Landlord is not responsible for items kept in it.

# 11. ENDING THE LEASE EARLY

Tenant may end this Lease before the end of the Term by giving at least 60 days' written notice and paying an early termination fee equal to two months' rent. The fee is due with the notice. Tenant remains responsible for rent up to the move-out date. A Tenant who is called to active military duty may end the Lease under the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act without the fee. Subletting the Premises or assigning this Lease is not allowed without Landlord's written consent, which Landlord will not unreasonably withhold.

# 12. MOVING OUT

Tenant must leave the Premises clean and return all keys, parking tags, and the storage locker key by noon on the last day. Landlord will inspect the Premises with Tenant if Tenant asks for an inspection at least 7 days before moving out. The charges in Schedule A apply to any item not left clean or in working order.

# 13. NOTICES

Notices to Landlord must be in writing and delivered by hand to the leasing office at 200 Maple Court, by mail, or through the resident portal. Notices to Tenant may be delivered to the Premises or through the resident portal.

# 14. QUIET HOURS AND RULES

Quiet hours are 10 p.m. to 7 a.m. Grills and open flames are not allowed on balconies; the shared gas grill by the pool may be used until 9 p.m. The pool is open from Memorial Day to Labor Day, 9 a.m. to 9 p.m., and guests must be accompanied by a resident. Smoking of any kind is not allowed anywhere in the building or within 25 feet of an entrance. A first violation of the smoking rule brings a written warning; a second brings a $250 fine.

# SCHEDULE A - MOVE-OUT CHARGES

These are the standard charges deducted from the security deposit when an item is not left clean or in working order at move-out. Damage beyond these items is charged at actual cost.
