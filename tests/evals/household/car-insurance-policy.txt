# RENTAL REIMBURSEMENT AND ROADSIDE ASSISTANCE

If the CR-V cannot be driven because of a covered loss, we pay for a rental car up to $40 per day, for no more than 30 days per loss, starting when the vehicle is left at the repair shop. If the CR-V is stolen, rental reimbursement starts 48 hours after you report the theft to us. Roadside assistance covers towing up to 15 miles to the nearest qualified repair shop, battery jump-starts, flat tire changes, fuel delivery (you pay for the fuel), and lockout service, limited to four service calls per vehicle per policy period. Call 1-800-555-0143 for roadside assistance.

# REPORTING A CLAIM

Report any accident or loss within 72 hours by calling 1-800-555-0142 (24 hours a day) or through the mobile app. At the scene: make sure everyone is safe and call the police if anyone is hurt; take photos of all vehicles, the damage, license plates, and the scene; get the other driver's name, phone number, insurer, and policy number; and do not admit fault. For theft, file a police report before calling us; we need the report number. For a hit-and-run, report it to the police within 24 hours for uninsured motorist coverage to apply.

You may choose any repair shop. If you use a shop in our Direct Repair network, we guarantee the repairs for as long as you own the vehicle and you do not need to get an estimate first. Otherwise, get one written estimate and send it with photos through the app.

# EXCLUSIONS

This policy does not cover: vehicles used to carry people or deliver goods for pay, such as ride-sharing or food delivery, unless the ride-share endorsement is added (it is not on this policy); racing or speed contests; damage from wear and tear, freezing, mechanical or electrical breakdown, or road salt corrosion; tires, unless damaged together with other covered loss; or any vehicle owned by a household member that is not listed above. Personal belongings stolen from the car, such as a laptop or phone, are not covered under this policy; they are covered under your renters or homeowners policy.

# PAYMENT AND CANCELLATION

Monthly installments are due on the 1st. If a payment is missed, we send a cancellation notice giving at least 10 days to pay before coverage ends. You may cancel at any time; the unearned premium is refunded minus a $25 cancellation fee. Adding a driver or vehicle takes effect when you call us, and is charged from that date.
